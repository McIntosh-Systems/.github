# McIntosh Systems organization profile

Public profile content and brand assets for [McIntosh Systems](https://github.com/McIntosh-Systems).

The organization overview is maintained in [`profile/README.md`](profile/README.md). Copy and imagery follow the [McIntosh Systems website](https://mcintoshsystems.com) and [brand guide](https://mcintoshsystems.com/brand).

The tonal blue rose is the current corporate mark. Preserve its geometry and colors; leave at least one petal of clear space around it. Brand assets are provided to identify McIntosh Systems, without granting trademark rights.
