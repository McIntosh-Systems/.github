<h1 align="center">Built around the business.</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Business consulting · Custom software · AI implementation</strong></p>

We build custom software and AI tools for operations and finance teams. From capital planning to spend analysis, we help teams replace manual work and make better use of their data.

### What we work on

- **Business processes:** requests, approvals, records, handoffs, and reporting.
- **Custom software and integrations:** applications built around the way your team works.
- **AI implementation:** tools that read documents, find answers, and prepare drafts inside everyday workflows.
- **Embedded engineering:** direct work with the people designing, building, and launching your software.

### Examples of what we build

| System | What it helps teams do |
| --- | --- |
| [CapFlow](https://mcintoshsystems.com/systems/capflow) | Plan capital investments and see what is requested, approved, and spent. |
| [SpendFlow](https://mcintoshsystems.com/systems/spendflow) | Understand purchasing, explore spend, and identify where to look for savings. |

We customize these systems to each team's processes, reporting needs, and existing tools. Our shared delivery platform, **McIntoshOS**, supports the applications we build.

We work with your team to understand where work gets stuck, simplify the process, and build the software to support it.
